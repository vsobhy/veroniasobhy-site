What drives it

Loving music is not enough anymore.

Not for indie artists. Being an artist today means being a brand: marketing, exposure, and identity, visual as much as musical. That is a second full time job, and it is not the one they signed up for.

I use AI to hand that grind back to the machine, so creators can outsource the entrepreneur work and stay on the part that ever mattered, the craft. It is the same instinct I bring to any team: spot the gap between the work and the business, and close it with the right build.
