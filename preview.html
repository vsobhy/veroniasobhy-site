Why it's personal
I am a musician, producer, and composer myself. So this is about protecting human artistry, not replacing it.

Being an artist today means being a brand: identity, marketing, exposure. It is a second full-time job, and not the one any of us signed up for. I use AI to hand that grind back to the machine, so creators can outsource the entrepreneur work and stay on the part they signed up for, their craft. Human-made, human-first. It is the same instinct I bring to any team: spot the gap between the work and the business, and close it with the right build.
